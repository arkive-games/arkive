# Glimmith tutorial projection

The local preview uses cooked Unreal 4.27 scene data from the installed game. No reference screenshots are used by the map or its markers.

- Export `OverworldMap`, `Overworld_Campaign`, `Welcome`, `Zone01_Intro_Bridge`, and `Zone01_Intro_Gate_Revised` as UAssetAPI JSON, alongside the corresponding packages under the original AGeri paths.
- Export the selected submap static meshes using CUE4Parse. The mesh JSON is keyed by imported object name. Each record has `vertices` as local-space XYZ triples from the first readable LOD. Include a flat triangle index array as `indices` for `SM_Scroll_001`, `SM_Plinth_V004`, `Ladder_SM`, and `Base_Ship_Full_Static_SM`. For the ship also include `sections` with `material` (the material interface object name), `FirstIndex`, and `NumTriangles`. Other meshes need only vertices.
- Run `node scripts/project-glimmith.mjs <AGeri directory> <assets directory> <mesh JSON>` with the 11 original puzzle files referenced by the bridge window. The generator derives the playable puzzle list from that window, plus three tutorial notes.

The opening view is rotated 59 degrees counterclockwise from the previous axis-aligned view to match the direction of travel in the reference view. It remains a plan view, not a recreation of the gameplay camera. Terrain and actor anchors use the same rotation. Raw XYZ values are preserved for reference.

The note and puzzle markers are simplified orthographic renders of `SM_Plinth_V004`. The note adds `SM_Scroll_001` at the tabletop height and orientation from `PuzzleTutorialCube`; the puzzle uses the piece defined in `0049.puz` as a generic glass inlay, not a solution or a per-puzzle texture. The web shading approximates the in-game materials. The front-facing stained-glass window icon is authored for map legibility and is not generated by the projection script. Difficulty badge PNGs are direct texture exports of `DifficultyIndicator_1` through `DifficultyIndicator_3` under `UI/Puzzles/Textures/RulePanelIcons/DifficultyIndicators`. The number comes from the puzzle's `DIFFICULTY` line.

Window coordinates, objective thresholds, and puzzle references come from `BP_StainedGlassPlinth_Bridge` and `BP_StainedGlassPlinth_Gate`. Chinese window names come from the matching `AreaName` keys in `Localization/Game/zh-Hans/Game.locres`. Prerequisite IDs outside the tutorial preview remain references, not new playable entries. Thresholds are authored values, not save progress.

`PuzzleCube_00Secret` (`0054`) declares `EUnlockCategory::GameCompletion`. `PuzzleCube_06` (`0527`) references it in `puzzleUnlocks`. The drawer presents the authored category and the separate reference, without claiming the two are an independently verified pair of conditions.

Map contours are convex projections of selected meshes, not a full terrain or collision reconstruction. The script rejects any puzzle or note marker whose anchor falls outside the projected contours near its height. Tutorial-1 is over `sm_RuinSetPiece_CircularA`. The bridge list also includes four optional puzzles: `0009` at the lower bridge ledge near a ladder, `0053` over `SM_Rock_01_cut`, `0012` over `SM_Flatrock_01`, and `0054` over `Base_Ship_Full_Static_SM` in Welcome. The view now covers the airship without moving any actor off its authored coordinates.

Welcome's boarding plank, dock planks, and four `SM_Bridge` sections retain their authored transforms and connect the ship to the tutorial island. The ship hull, deck, cabin, and port and starboard wing sails use separate footprints to avoid covering the open air between them; the overhead balloon is deliberately omitted from the floor plan. The ladder layer uses the projected mesh triangles to keep gaps between rungs. Floating rocks, walkable-looking surfaces, and open air use contrasting colors, without drawing routes over gaps or claiming to track unlocked traversal.
